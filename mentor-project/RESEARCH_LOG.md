## Phase 9 research (2026-10-16)
- Correctness vs faithfulness confirmed from the PRIMARY paper (Wallat et al., ICTIR 2025, ACM DOI 10.1145/3731120.3744592; preprint arXiv 2412.18004): prior work evaluates citation correctness (cited doc supports statement); faithfulness (genuine reliance vs post-rationalized to fit prior knowledge) is a separate property; experiment on Command-R+ finds up to 57% of citations post-rationalized. Limits noted: single-model analysis, trust-impact on users unmeasured. Consequence: our validator enforces validity (existence/containment, V1–V8); faithfulness approximated by V4 quote-check + abstention + B3−B6 gap, support measured Phase 22. [PRIMARY RESEARCH]
- Injection-delimiting limits confirmed from OWASP LLM01:2025/2026 (genai.owasp.org): no fool-proof prevention exists (stochastic core; LLMs make no architectural instruction/data distinction); RAG/fine-tuning do not fully mitigate; controls are defense-in-depth — constrain role, define + validate output schema in TRUSTED code, segregate external content BUT labeling alone does not enforce the boundary. Consequence: delimiters in prompts.py are one layer; the validator + no-tool Q&A path + audit are the enforcement. [OFFICIAL]
- Entailment limits: NLI-as-fact-check rests on semantic-necessity datasets (SNLI/MNLI) that generalize poorly; FactRel/StarSEM 2024 shows 84% of factually-supporting news pairs are NOT NLI entailment and 63% of undermining pairs not contradiction; RefNLI (Findings NAACL 2025) shows retrieved premises break reference determinacy (>80% false contradiction). Consequence recorded as the honest limit: validator proves containment, never semantic entailment. [PRIMARY RESEARCH]
- Prompt finding (own measurement): pasting the full Pydantic JSON schema ($defs/titles) into the system prompt makes Qwen3-4B echo the schema verbatim instead of answering; compact shape + one example restores 22/22 shape-validity. max-tokens 256 truncates JSON mid-object → 1024 for answer calls. [INFERENCE from measurement]
